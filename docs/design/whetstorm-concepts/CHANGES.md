# Whetstorm concept art

These are original concept pictures for review and future 3D modeling. They are
not game-ready models and are not wired into the client. See
`docs/whetstorm/concept-art-generation.json` for prompts, dimensions and hashes.

- `old-peatfire.png`: full-body, front-facing dragon-kin concept. The hands sit
  close to the image edges; a modeler may want extra margin and side/back views.
- `bulwark-spear.png`: one-handed leaf-bladed starter spear.
- `bulwark-shield.png`: tall wooden shield with iron bands and stone boss.
- `brute-great-axe.png`: two-handed crescent axe.

No 3D generation or Tripo job has been run. Designs need owner review and actual
3D construction, rigging where needed, and in-game validation before use.
