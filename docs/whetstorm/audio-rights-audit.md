### Temporal clock selection and replacement

`scripts/sfx/sfx_prompts.mjs` declares `temporal_clock` as a custom recording without a generation prompt. The runtime pack lists one main variant, `/audio/sfx/temporal_clock.mp3?v=01240b109c87`, with SHA-256 `01240b109c87a07bca05e2bf7eae23ea597bef270b4110bcf3d9338d56824100`; `scripts/sfx/sfx_gain_ceiling.generated.json` records the same hash. `src/game/sfx.ts` loads the validated whole-catalog runtime pack and fetches its selected variant. If the pack is unavailable or invalid, `src/game/sfx_runtime_pack.ts` falls back to the compiled `SFX_CLIPS` manifest. That generated manifest and the recording are absent in this partial worktree.

Changing the runtime-pack URL alone does not remove the compiled fallback. A replacement needs a suitable independently sourced or synthesized cue with recorded rights and provenance, SFX conformance and listening review, and regeneration of the compiled manifest and runtime pack through the owning SFX pipeline, including their catalog and bundle identities. Verify both selection paths and the resulting complete package. No cleared clock-like substitute was established in this audit: nearby countdown and loot cues are custom recordings whose individual source and permission were not established here. No audio was created or modified.

An independent local synthesis route is available without a paid service: `scripts/sfx/ui_sfx.mjs` defines layered tone and noise recipes, and `scripts/gen_ui_sfx.mjs` renders a lossless intermediate with FFmpeg before conforming it to `public/audio/sfx/<key>.mp3`. There is currently no `temporal_clock` recipe, so one must be authored and reconciled with the existing custom catalog entry in `scripts/sfx/sfx_prompts.mjs` without creating duplicate keys or accidentally changing its gain and conform policy. The generator skips existing outputs unless forced; any replacement should target the single intended cue. The pinned FFmpeg and FFprobe binaries exist in this worktree's `node_modules`; their execution and codec support were not checked in this audit, and the UI generator defaults to a PATH `ffmpeg` unless passed `--ffmpeg`.

Review the new sound by ear and verify the mono 44.1 kHz, 192 kbps MP3, duration-dependent loudness and true-peak rules, and strict naming and channel policy using the SFX conformance tools. Then `node scripts/build_sfx_manifest.mjs` measures custom gain ceilings and regenerates both the compiled manifest and runtime pack. Validate the cue through both selection paths and in the final package. **Do not regenerate the complete catalog from this partial checkout:** its audio binaries are missing, so doing so could omit unrelated entries. Use a complete, controlled asset inventory and verify the resulting coverage.
