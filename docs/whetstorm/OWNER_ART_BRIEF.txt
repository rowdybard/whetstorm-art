The style line

Send this as the first message of a new ChatGPT chat, then send the prompts below in that same chat, so every picture looks like the same game. It is a first draft of Whetstorm's look: change any words you like, then keep using your version everywhere.

For every picture in this chat, use the Whetstorm art style: original stylized fantasy with a hand-painted look, bold clean shapes and strong silhouettes, slightly heroic proportions and oversized weapons, warm sunlit sandstone, worn leather and forged steel against slate-blue storm skies, a crackling white-blue lightning accent, soft painterly light from the upper left. No text, no letters, no logos, no watermark. An original design that does not copy or imitate any existing game, film or artist.

If a picture drifts off style, paste the line again and say "closer to the style line".

Prompt 2: the login frame (1)

This is the border drawn around the login box. Its band widths matter, because the game stretches only the middle.

An ornate frame for a game's login window, PNG, exactly 1024 pixels wide and 1536 pixels tall, front view, flat and symmetrical. Dark forged iron and worn bronze with grey whetstone inlay and thin glowing white-blue lightning lines, and a small crossed-blades crest at the top centre. The bands are thick: the top band is about 400 pixels tall, each side band about 280 pixels wide, and the bottom band about 250 pixels tall. The whole middle inside the frame is empty and fully transparent, and so is everything outside the frame.
