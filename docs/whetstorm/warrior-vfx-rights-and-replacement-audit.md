# Warrior VFX rights and replacement audit

Read-only source and reference-checkout inspection. The partial art worktree lacks the inherited runtime binaries; `D:/action-mmo` contains the contact KTX2 files and production sheets discussed below. Presence in that checkout does not establish downstream package contents or redistribution terms. No in-game replacement fit has been verified.

## Contact sheets

`src/render/ability_vfx/contact_assets.ts` requests `/textures/vfx/contact/cut.ktx2`, `crush.ktx2`, and `pierce.ktx2` on demand with the Warrior kit. No exact credit row establishing the creator or terms of these files was found in `CREDITS.md`; unrecorded terms are not a finding that they are explicitly permission-required. `docs/whetstorm/contact-vfx-candidates.md` records independent procedural 2048px RGBA 8-by-8 sources with transparent gutters and blank endpoints. They are **unshipped candidates**, not current runtime replacements. That note records the existing runtime format as one-level, sRGB, alpha-bearing UASTC KTX2 and identifies the need for a verified encoder.

Before selecting a replacement, verify cell order, vertical orientation through encode/decode, alpha and edge bleed, GPU memory, the on-demand loader and active-kit prewarm. Inspect early, middle, and late frames under the actual shader, including reduced-motion, HDR and low-range output. The source tests and binary-introduction history do not establish those results or the inherited files' rights.

## Production flipbooks and fragments

`src/render/ability_vfx/production_assets.ts` separately loads production WebP sheets for smoke, shout dust, Warrior power, fervor, harvest impact, bite, shear, and shockwave, the `warrior_crush.ktx2` sheet, and `public/models/vfx/production_fragments.glb`. These are separate from the contact KTX2 files above. No exact credit row was found for the entire family, so inspect each source and rights record separately; do not label every member permission-required. For example, `scripts/assets/vfx_production/bake_warrior_crush.py` describes an original procedural bake without external assets, while `resize_generic_sheets.mjs` says the frame sources for smoke, shout dust, shockwave, and harvest impact are not committed. The Whetstorm pressure, blood-film, and forged-steel textures under `public/textures/vfx/whetstorm/` are separately documented original replacements in `docs/whetstorm/warrior-material-textures-provenance.md`; they do not clear the other production sheets.

Read-only metadata inspection of the reference `warrior_power.webp` and `warrior_fervor.webp` found both 2048-by-2048 RGBA. `src/render/ability_vfx/baked_impact_layers.ts` samples 64 cells in an 8-by-8 grid with reversed texture-V rows, conditionally mirrors X, blends adjacent frames in premultiplied space and outputs straight alpha with normal blending. For power sheets it uses a 4/256 cell gutter and pivot `(0.5 - (1.45 / 5.6) * mirror, 0.5 + 0.8 / 5.6)`; reduced motion fixes the frame phase at 0.36. Preserve or deliberately adapt this sampling contract, including transparency and timing. Example calls include a 0.14-second release and a 0.52-second cast (`fury_release.ts`, `warrior_power_cast.ts`). Generic fallback presentation is not evidence of pixel-equivalent output. Verify the new sheet in actual encounter and graphics-tier rendering, prewarm/loading behavior, and performance before shipping.
