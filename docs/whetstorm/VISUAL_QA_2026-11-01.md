# Sampled visual art review — 2026-11-01

Art-branch snapshot: `ae3e3a6e8a` (after the Eastbrook Greatsword correction).
This is a bounded visual review against current item names, slots, weapon
types and the descriptions available in source. It is not a full asset,
licensing, model-parity, acquisition, browser or release audit. A match here
means no clear subject or slot mismatch was found in the sampled art, not
that a picture has been approved in every game context.

Original item masters are under `docs/design/whetstorm-art/items/` and their
delivery images under `public/ui/whetstorm/items/`; follow the actual per-item
art mapping and provenance rather than assuming all artwork is in one batch.
Some weapon extremities sit near image edges without being cropped. Visual
details may be lost at inventory size, and in-game rendering remains to be
checked in a complete candidate.

Five early deed crests were also sampled: `prog_first_steps`,
`prog_first_planting`, `prog_first_craft`, `cmb_first_blood`, and
`prog_first_harvest`. Their mapped files exist. First Harvest applies to any
gathering node, while its leafy illustration especially suggests plants; this
is an illustrative ambiguity, not evidence that the deed grants incorrectly.

The eight `bulwark_*` launch artwork IDs are presentation entries; this
review did not find corresponding simulation ability definitions. Do not
count those pictures as proof of playable skills. Nine sampled defined
hunter, paladin and priest abilities had existing mapped images and no clear
subject mismatch: `pack_command`, `frostjaw_trap`, `arcane_shot`, `holy_light`,
`hammer_of_grace`, `divine_protection`, `smite`, `power_word_shield`, `renew`.
